# Cap each response's reasoning at 8K tokens, and don't let a cut or looping response end the run

hypothesis: Now a single response can think for 20K–113K tokens. Almost always the model is trying to recall an upstream fix or a list of identifiers from memory, and that often turns into a loop of counting numbers. Such a response either burns many minutes, or ends with the intended tool call buried inside its reasoning (or cut at the output cap). Then there is no real tool call, so the run stops with whatever is on disk, sometimes nothing. With `thinkingBudgets.medium = 8192`, the server ends reasoning at 8K tokens and the model goes on to answer. An extension (`runaway-guard.ts`) covers the cases where that still goes wrong. If the last response ended reasoning-only, was cut at the length limit, wrote its tool call as text, or hit the budget and made no call, `agent_before_settle` adds one short message and asks for another turn (at most 2 in a row and 4 per run). A thinking or text block that was cut at the length limit, or that loops, is trimmed to its first 2,000 characters at `message_end` so it doesn't sit in every later request. In any repo, this bounds the cost of one bad response, and the run keeps working instead of ending empty.

evidence: In the parent's 1,990 responses, 11 had more than 8,192 output tokens, nearly all of it thinking,, spread over 9 runs (3 passed, 6 failed): django-11477 (34K/53K/15K), 13033 (10K), 13089 (25K), 14404 (113K), 15037 (23K), matplotlib-22871 (23K), sphinx-7985 (17K), sympy-14248 (79K) and 15976 (50K). Every one of them was recall or a loop ("let me search my memory", "the instances include 11618, 11890, …"), not design work. Those 11 responses hold 444K of the batch's 1.49M output tokens, about 354K of it beyond the 8K cap; in the 3 passed runs they were the same kind of ramble and only cost time. Three runs ended on such a response with no real tool call, and all three failed: in matplotlib-22871 and sympy-14248 (both diverged tasks, passing in 2/3 and 1/3 runs) the counting loop ended in `</tool_call>` inside the reasoning, so the run stopped with no diff. django-14404 was cut at the output cap after 113K tokens. These are the only 2 no-diff runs and the only output-cap run in the batch. Responses of 4K–8K tokens include some real design work (16454 t2, 13974 t9/t18), so the cap isn't set lower. Tries: with a 100-token budget the model made a tool call after 4 of 5 forced cuts, and the guard handled the fifth. A live replay of a thinking-only looping response was trimmed, nudged once, and the run went on to edit, verify and finish. A normal run stayed quiet.

predicts: No response's reasoning goes over ~8.2K tokens. Runs that end on a reasoning-only or length-cut response go from about 3 in 75 to about 0, and so do no-diff runs caused by them. "acted" should show "reasoning hit the budget" on roughly 10–15% of runs and a continuation on a few percent. Expect equal or slightly more passes (the empty endings on tasks that other runs pass) and clearly fewer output tokens and minutes in the heavy tail, about 15–25% of total output tokens. It's wrong if passes drop (cut reasoning hurting fixes), if output tokens don't fall because the runaway carries on into visible text (length endings with "trimmed" entries), or if most continuations come right after real final answers and change nothing.
